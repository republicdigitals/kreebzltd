# Kreebz Ltd — Product Marketing Context

## What Kreebz does
Premium real estate company in Lagos, Nigeria. Services:
- Property sales & leasing (curated/vetted portfolio, off-market inventory)
- Facility & estate management (owner representation, not just maintenance)
- Concierge & lifestyle services (matchmaking, reservations, lifestyle orchestration)
- Private jet charter (light/midsize/heavy, Paystack checkout live)
- Development advisory + the Bourdillon, Ikoyi project (own development, foundation stage)

## Audiences (all matter, in this priority)
1. Diaspora Nigerians — buying/investing remotely, need trust + verification
2. Lagos HNWIs — time-poor, want one accountable contact
3. Property owners/landlords — need management, marketing, selling
4. Corporate/investor clients — acquisitions, portfolio management

## Voice
- Warm & personal; conversational standard English (NO Nigerian pidgin — user confirmed)
- Simple over complex, active voice, confident but never sensational
- Second person ("you/your"), short sentences, one idea per section

## Primary conversion goal
Get the visitor to reach out — enquiry form, concierge request, call/WhatsApp.
Secondary: browse portfolio, book the jet.

## Key differentiators (user-confirmed)
- Vetted, curated listings only — not a marketplace
- Diaspora-safe process — remote verification, transparent docs
- Full-stack lifestyle — property + management + concierge + jets under one roof
- One trusted point of contact — a principal, not a ticket queue
